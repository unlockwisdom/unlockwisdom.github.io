Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers the private uploader apps "Champ vs Challenger Uploader" and "Unlock Wisdom Uploader" (the "Apps").

Who uses the Apps

Only the channel owner. The Apps are not offered to the public and no other person signs in to them.

What data the Apps use

With the owner's Google sign-in, the Apps use the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, manage playlists, edit titles and descriptions, and read and reply to comments on the owner's own channels. The Apps do not collect data about other people, and do not sell, share or transfer any data.

YouTube API Services

The Apps use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoking it stops all use and the stored tokens are deleted.
